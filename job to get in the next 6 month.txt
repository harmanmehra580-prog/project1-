# 🇦🇪 Important UAE work visa

If you're applying from India, you don't necessarily need to arrange a normal employment visa independently before getting hired.

For private-sector employment, the employer normally handles the employment work-permit process. UAE government guidance says the process involves a formal job offer, employment contract, work permit and then the work visaresidence process. chatgpt-content-reference{index=2}

The UAE also provides a standard employment residence visa for employees, with the employer applying for the standard work visa. chatgpt-content-reference{index=3}

Never pay someone simply because they claim they can guarantee a Dubai job or work visa. Verify any offer through official UAE channels; MoHRE provides an offercontract inquiry service. chatgpt-content-reference{index=4}
